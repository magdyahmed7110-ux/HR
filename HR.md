# 👥 HR Analytics Dashboard (Power BI)

An interactive Power BI dashboard that gives HR teams a clear overview of the workforce: headcount, salaries, hiring trends, and employee turnover.

## Key Metrics
- Total Employees
- Total Salary
- Salary by Department and City
- Employment Status breakdown

## Visuals
- **Employees by Termination Reason** (donut chart)
- **Salary by City and Department** (bar chart)
- **Hires by Month** (area chart)
- **Salary by Quarter and Employment Status** (column chart)

## Filters (Slicers)
Employment Status · Gender · Hire Date · Year

## Data Model
Main `Hrd` table linked to `Employees` and `Department Bridge` tables.

## Tools
- Power BI Desktop
- DAX / Power Query

## How to Open
Download `HR-Assal.pbix` and open it with Power BI Desktop.
